Our Story

Founded in 2021 in Chennai, HomeFinder+ began with a mission to modernize and simplify digital experiences. What started as a small passion project has grown into a fully scaled company with thousands of active users. We specialize in product design, clean UI/UX, modern websites and future-ready digital tools.

Our culture is built on learning, creativity, transparency, and delivering truly meaningful design to users.
