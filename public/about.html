Our story

From a Chennai side project to a nationwide platform

Founded in 2021 in Chennai, HomeFinder+ began with a mission to modernize and simplify the way people find homes. What started as a small passion project has grown into a platform with thousands of active users.

Our culture is built on learning, creativity, transparency and delivering truly meaningful design.

Our mission

To build user-first products that blend aesthetics, speed, intelligence and simplicity — interfaces that feel alive, intuitive and deeply human.

2021 — Beginnings

Started as a small design & development studio.

2022 — Expansion

Grew into a multi-product company with thousands of users.

2023 — New direction

Shifted focus to interactive, immersive and smart experiences.

2024 — Innovation

Building smarter search, direct owner messaging and better tools for owners.
